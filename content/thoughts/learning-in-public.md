## Why Publish Technical Notes?

Publishing rough notes in public forces clarity.

When you know someone else might read your setup instructions for a reverse proxy, you test the commands. When you document why an `iptables` rule dropped packets, you understand the Linux network stack more deeply.

### Feedback Loops

1. **Write it down**: Solidify what you learned.
2. **Publish statically**: Keep it fast and archive-ready.
3. **Iterate**: Refine as configurations improve.
