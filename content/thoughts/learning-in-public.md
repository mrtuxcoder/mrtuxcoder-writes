## Why Publish Technical Notes?

Publishing technical notes in public forces clarity and rigor.

When you know an article may be referenced by someone attempting the same reverse-proxy or container setup, you test every shell command. When you document why an `iptables` rule dropped incoming traffic, you end up studying the Linux kernel packet traversal model far more deeply.

### Feedback Loops

1. **Write it down**: Document findings immediately while details are fresh.
2. **Compile statically**: Ensure content remains permanent and archive-friendly.
3. **Iterate**: Refine documentation as production architectures evolve.
